📊 P1400 HR Analytics

📌 Project Overview

P1400 HR Analytics is a data analytics project focused on exploring employee data to understand 
workforce distribution, employee attrition, employee demographics, and other HR trends.

This project uses Microsoft Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau to organize, analyze, and visualize HR data.
The goal is to transform raw employee data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven human resource decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.
- Understand employee distribution across departments.
- Explore employee demographics and job roles.
- Analyze salary and monthly income trends.
- Identify patterns in employee data.
- Develop dashboards and visual reports.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making.

Attrition Rate Formula

Attrition Rate = (Number of Employees Who Left ÷ Total Employees) × 100

The employee population and reporting period should be defined consistently when calculating this metric.
